## Depth pass (2026-10-21): the four admin screens rebuilt to the depth standard

Pass: depth (wave 6 of the depth program).
Method: the three gates and the per-module contract of
`docs/audit/FLOW_AND_LAYOUT_AUDIT_20261021.md` (gate A truth, gate B flow, gate C layout),
applied to the admin screens of this module. Every claim below was read out of source on
2026-10-21 and is cited as `file:line`.
Live file: `docs/audit/features/assets.md` (the open rows and the closed rows of this pass).

This pass found the earlier claim of this file ("WORKING") to be a component-presence claim and
not a flow claim: the screens had the parts and not the behaviour. The list kept its filters in
local state, the numbers never named their scope, the office could not act on an asset without a
detour, the detail page showed a write trail of two timestamps, and no page said which actions
reach the residents of a room and which do not.
