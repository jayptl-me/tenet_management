### 2. What the operator is doing here

The operator answers the absence requests residents filed. What is at stake is not a number of
requests but two things: the resident is waiting for an answer, and the answer writes the
attendance board, which is what the office sees and what the resident is marked against. The rent is
not part of it: the bill is raised from the resident own record (apps/api/src/services/invoice.service.ts:97),
so no leave changes what anybody owes. Done looks like: every request carries a decision, the
approved days are marked on the attendance board, and the resident has been told the outcome and the
reason.
